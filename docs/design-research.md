# Design research and UI provenance

This redesign uses original HTML, CSS, JavaScript and generated editorial imagery composed for Upholstery Warehouse. Hertex product names, public prices and canonical links are used as supplier catalogue references; Hertex branding, marketing copy and photography are not reproduced.

## Uiverse

The loader, luminous border treatments, hover sheen and layered card motion were adapted from interaction patterns studied in the [Uiverse element library](https://uiverse.io/), including its card, button and loader collections. Uiverse publishes its UI elements under the MIT License. The final components were rewritten to fit this site's accessible markup, reduced-motion behavior and Workshop Gold design system.

## Catalogue and merchandising references

- [Hertex](https://www.hertex.co.za/) informed the emphasis on large imagery, collection-led discovery and material-first navigation.
- Named catalogue references link to the official [Outdoor Plains](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/Outdoor%20Plains), [La Med](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/La%20Med), [Matador](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/Matador), [Rewild](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/Rewild), [French Flair](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/French%20Flair), [Regatta](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/Regatta) and [The Great Migration](https://www.hertex.co.za/item_group/005/collection/The%20Great%20Migration) collections.
- Furniture references link back to the official [Hertex HAUS furniture catalogue](https://hertexhaus.co.za/collections/homeware-furniture). The 35-piece local edit records public supplier prices checked on 29 September 2026 and sends shoppers to the live supplier page for variants, stock and purchase.
- Upholstery references covering flat weaves, jacquards, chenille, bouclé, velvet, wool, linen, coated textiles, outdoor fabrics and technical surfaces informed the taxonomy. Every entry is presented as an illustrative material direction; the site avoids unverified stock, performance and supplier claims.
- [Baymard product-list research](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists) informed the visible filtering, sorting, product-count and scan-friendly card hierarchy. Its [furniture ecommerce research](https://baymard.com/research/houseware-and-furnishings) informed the mobile controls and product-detail handoff.
- Furniture research expanded the shop across sofas, armchairs, dining, bedroom, outdoor, office, stools, ottomans, tables and storage.

No Hertex product or fabric photography was copied or hot-linked. The shop uses six original generated editorial scenes stored under `public/assets/store/`; every product surface calls the image an editorial category visual and links to the supplier for the exact item. The generated prompts are recorded in the implementation task history and contain no brand names or existing product designs.
